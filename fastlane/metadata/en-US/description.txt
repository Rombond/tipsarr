Tipsarr is the companion app of Tipsarr, a free and open-source server you host yourself next to Jellyfin. Browse what is popular, see at a glance what your server already has, and ask for what it lacks.

IMPORTANT: Tipsarr needs a Tipsarr server (self-hosted, MIT licence) connected to a Jellyfin server. There is no Tipsarr account or cloud service: you sign in to your own server with your Jellyfin account. No server yet? Try the public demo at https://tipsarr-demo.brebond.com.

DISCOVER
- Trending, popular and upcoming movies and shows, genres, people, collections and trailers
- Personal suggestions built from your Jellyfin watch history ("Because you watched…")
- Weekend and weekly box-office charts
- Availability badges: see instantly what is already on your server

REQUEST AND FOLLOW
- Request a movie or a show (choose the seasons), with quality profile and folder when allowed
- Follow progress live: requested, approved, downloading, available
- Admins and request managers approve or decline from the app
- Report a problem with a video, audio or subtitles and follow the discussion

YOUR SPACE
- Watchlist and "not interested" list
- Profile with your viewing statistics
- Several servers and accounts on one device
- Push notifications when a request is approved or available (when the server admin enables them)
- Teasers: a vertical feed of trailers (experimental, when enabled on the server)
- English and French, light and dark themes, phones and tablets

PRIVACY
No analytics, no advertising, no tracking. The app only talks to your own server. Nothing is ever requested or downloaded automatically: requests go through your server admin.
